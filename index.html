Radar Trace

Aviation accidents and incidents, reconstructed from the official investigation reports.

Radar Trace is a YouTube channel. Each video follows the final report of an official investigation authority, such as the NTSB, the AAIB or the ATSB, and every figure on screen cites the report and page it comes from. The videos never show victims, name passengers or animate the impact.

The Radar Trace publishing tool

The channel uses a private tool, run only by the channel owner, to upload its own videos, thumbnails and subtitles to its own YouTube channel and to read that channel's statistics. It uses YouTube API Services. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.
