Privacy policy

Radar Trace publishing tool. Last updated: November 6, 2026.

Who this applies to

The Radar Trace publishing tool is a private application used only by the owner of the Radar Trace YouTube channel, to manage that channel. It has no other users and collects no data from viewers or from the public.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

With the permission the channel owner grants through Google sign-in, the tool:

  • uploads videos, thumbnails and subtitles to the owner's own channel, and schedules their publication;
  • reads public information about videos, to avoid repeating topics other channels have covered;
  • reads the YouTube Analytics reports of the owner's own videos: views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained, likes, shares and traffic sources.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to learn which videos work best. The authorization token and the statistics are stored on the channel owner's own server, readable only by the owner. They are not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while the channel is active. The owner can delete them, and the authorization token, at any time from the server. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings. After a revocation, the owner deletes the token and the statistics from the server.
